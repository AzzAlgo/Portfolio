## ★ Engagement 01 — PMO Cost Manager · Advanced Modular Reactor programme

`Nuclear · Generation-IV reactors`
**Role:** PMO — Cost Manager
**Budget:** > **1 B€**
**Duration:** 12 months
**Country:** France
**Year:** 2024–2025

**Context.** Cost-management mission on the lead programme of an
innovator in Generation-IV Advanced Modular Reactors — lead-cooled,
fuelled by reprocessed nuclear waste.

**Contribution.** Built and operated the cost-management discipline
across the programme lifecycle. Authored the Cost Management Plan and
Cost Estimate Plan in alignment with contractual requirements.
Structured cost and resource allocation against the WBS, ran variance
analysis (CPI / SPI / EVM), maintained budget forecasts, and produced
executive financial reports. In the final three months, took over the
risk discipline — deployed the risk-management process via a PowerApps
tool, maintained the Risk Register and Response Plans, facilitated risk
workshops with stakeholders, and produced Risk Reports aligned with
cost and schedule.

**Outcome.** Structured and secured cost piloting on a > 1 B€
programme. Materially improved financial visibility and the quality
of strategic decision-making at programme management level.
